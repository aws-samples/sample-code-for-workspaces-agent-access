# Task: Draw a Stick Figure Dog in MS Paint

Draw a simple stick figure dog using lines and ovals. Keep it simple.

You may take up to 15 screenshots. Use them whenever you need to check your progress.

---

## Step 1: Launch Paint

1. Click the search bar on the taskbar, type "paint"
2. Click "Paint" in the search results (classic Paint, not Paint 3D)
3. Take a screenshot to see the canvas. If most of the canvas isn't visible, maximize or increase the size of the window first.

---

## Step 2: Draw the Dog

Don't worry about setting color/outline/fill dropdowns — just use the default tool settings.

### Body
1. Select the **Oval shape tool** from the Shapes section
2. Draw a horizontal oval in the center of the canvas for the body

### Head
3. Draw a smaller circle to the left of the body, slightly overlapping

### Legs
4. Select the **Line tool** from the Shapes section
5. Draw 4 short vertical lines down from the bottom of the body oval

### Tail
6. Draw a short diagonal line going up-right from the right end of the body

Take a screenshot to check your drawing. It's okay if the wrong tools were used as long as the drawing looks close enough.

---

## Step 3: Save

1. Press **Ctrl+S**
2. If a Save As dialog appears:
   - Click the "File name" field
   - Type **"dog"**
   - Click **Save**
3. Take a screenshot to confirm save

---

## If Something Goes Wrong
- Press **Escape** to close any unexpected dialog
- Press **Ctrl+Z** to undo mistakes
- Take a screenshot if you're unsure what state things are in
